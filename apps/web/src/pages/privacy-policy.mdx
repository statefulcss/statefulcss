## 2. Information That Is Processed

### 2.1 Technical Data

When you visit this Website, certain technical information is processed to deliver the content
to your browser:

- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Device type
- Date and time of access
- Pages requested
- Referring URL (if any)
- Country/region (derived from IP address)

### 2.2 Email Communication

If you contact us via email, we will process the personal data contained in your correspondence
(such as your email address and message content) to respond to your inquiry.

## 3. Service Providers

### Cloudflare (Hosting and Security)

This Website is hosted on **Cloudflare**. Cloudflare provides website hosting, content
delivery, DNS services, and security protection.

Cloudflare processes technical data (including IP addresses) to deliver content and protect
the Website. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Cloudflare
provides safeguards through Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the EU-US Data Privacy
Framework.

For details, see: [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

### mailbox.org (Email)

Emails sent to statefulcss.com addresses are received and stored by **mailbox.org** (Heinlein Hosting
GmbH, Berlin, Germany), which processes them on our behalf in data centres in Berlin. Your messages are
not processed outside the European Union.

For details, see: [mailbox.org Privacy Policy](https://mailbox.org/en/data-protection/)

### Cloudflare Web Analytics

We use **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to understand how many people visit this Website, which pages
they read, which websites refer them here, and how quickly pages load. A small script loaded from
`static.cloudflareinsights.com` reports page views, the referring website, page load and performance
timings, and general information about device type, browser, operating system, and country to
Cloudflare.

According to Cloudflare, Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage and does not track
individual visitors across websites. We only see aggregated statistics.

For details, see: [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/)

Apart from this script, fonts and all other assets are served from this Website itself. The Website
does not use advertising services or other third-party content.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the legal bases for processing are:

- **Legitimate Interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for website hosting and security. Processing is
  necessary to deliver the Website and protect it against abuse.
- **Legitimate Interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for website analytics. Aggregated, cookieless
  statistics help us understand which content is useful and keep the Website fast, without
  identifying individual visitors.
- **Legitimate Interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for responding to email inquiries.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest (see Section 7).

## 5. Data Retention

- **Cloudflare**: Retention periods are detailed in their [Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics**: Detailed data is kept for 7 days; aggregated statistics are available for 6 months
- **Email correspondence**: Retained as long as necessary for the purpose of the communication

## 6. Automated Decision-Making

No automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR takes place.

## 7. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are in the European Union, you have the following rights:

- **Right to information** about the processing of your personal data
- **Right of access** to your personal data
- **Right to rectification** of inaccurate personal data
- **Right to erasure** ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to restriction** of processing
- **Right to data portability**
- **Right to object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Right to lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority

To exercise your rights, contact us at: [info@statefulcss.com](mailto:info@statefulcss.com)

**Note**: Due to the privacy-preserving setup of this Website, we do not have access to data that
could identify individual visitors. If you have concerns, please contact us and we will do our
best to assist.

## 8. Cookies

This Website does not set cookies and does not store anything in your browser.

Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies in exceptional cases, for example while
mitigating an attack. These are exempt from consent requirements under GDPR.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Cloudflare operates a global network. Your request may be processed by a Cloudflare server
outside the EEA. Cloudflare provides appropriate safeguards through Standard Contractual
Clauses (SCCs) and EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification.

Emails are processed exclusively in Germany by mailbox.org.
